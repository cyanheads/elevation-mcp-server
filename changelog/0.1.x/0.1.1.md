# 0.1.1 — 2026-10-01

## Added

- **`elevation_get_points`** — ground elevation at 1–100 `{lat, lon}` points in meters and feet, each with its `dataset` and `resolution_m`; 3DEP answers add `raster_id` and `acquisition_date`, and a point without data comes back `no_data` instead of failing the call.
- **`elevation_get_profile`** — samples a route of 2–1,000 vertices at 2–250 evenly spaced points (default 100) and summarizes distance, ascent, descent, elevation range, highest and lowest points, and steepest grades; `include_samples: false` returns the summary without per-sample rows ([#1](https://github.com/cyanheads/elevation-mcp-server/issues/1)).
- **`elevation_get_grid`** — samples a node grid (`rows` and `cols` 2–25, at most 250 nodes) over a south/west/north/east box and returns elevation and per-cell dataset matrices, the highest and lowest nodes, mean elevation, and relief.
- **`elevation_check_line_of_sight`** — a `clear`, `blocked`, or `indeterminate` verdict for the sightline between an observer and a target up to 1,000 km apart, with minimum clearance, the limiting point, and the first obstruction; `earth_model` is `flat`, `geometric`, `optical` (default), or `radio`.
- **`source`** on every tool — `auto` (default) queries USGS 3DEP inside its coverage and sends 3DEP misses and every other point to Open Topo Data (`srtm30m`, then `mapzen`); `usgs_3dep` or `opentopodata` pins one provider. Only a coverage miss falls back, never an outage, and neither provider needs an API key.
- **Upstream pacing** — 3DEP at 6 lookups in flight and 10 a second per process; the public Open Topo Data instance at 100 points per request, one request at a time, 1.1 s apart, and at most 1,000 per trailing 24 hours. Each call has a 45 s sampling budget.
- **Failure reasons** — `usgs_unavailable`, `opentopodata_unavailable`, `opentopodata_rate_limited`, `opentopodata_daily_limit`, `opentopodata_config_rejected`, and `sampling_deadline_exceeded` on every tool, each with recovery text naming the next call.
- **`OPENTOPODATA_BASE_URL`** — points the server at a self-hosted Open Topo Data instance, run at 4 requests in flight with no rate windows; it must serve `srtm30m` and `mapzen` and accept 100 locations per request. Unset or blank means the public instance.
- **Attribution** — every result's `Sources:` line credits each dataset that answered: USGS 3DEP and SRTM GL1 v3 (public domain), plus Mapzen terrain tiles' full multi-source attribution whenever a Mapzen value is returned.
