# 0.1.2 — 2026-10-01

## Added

- **`water_surface_m`** on `elevation_check_line_of_sight` — optional water level (−500 to 9,000 m): every sample's surface, endpoints included, becomes the higher of its elevation and that level, replacing the default that lifts only Mapzen sea-floor values to 0 m ([#3](https://github.com/cyanheads/elevation-mcp-server/issues/3)).
- **`frequency_mhz`** on `elevation_check_line_of_sight` — optional (30–300,000 MHz); adds `fresnel`, a `sufficient`, `insufficient`, or `indeterminate` verdict against 60% of the first Fresnel zone, with `min_clearance_ratio` and the limiting sample's `fresnel_radius_m`. A `clear` line short of 60% gets a notice ([#4](https://github.com/cyanheads/elevation-mcp-server/issues/4)).
- **Public hosted endpoint** — `https://elevation.caseyjhand.com/mcp` (Streamable HTTP), listed under `remotes` in `server.json` and in the README's Public Hosted Instance section.

## Changed

- **`elevation_get_profile` coarse-spacing notice** — now also fires at the 250-sample cap, where it advises splitting the route into shorter calls and summing their ascent and descent ([#5](https://github.com/cyanheads/elevation-mcp-server/issues/5)).
- **README** — capability reference, features, and known limitations tightened; the hosted-deployment limits now read as shared-deployment limits.

## Fixed

- **`elevation_check_line_of_sight` over USGS 3DEP values below 0 m** — a notice names those samples (bay floor or land below sea level, measured as received) and points to `water_surface_m`; the server instructions now say 3DEP can carry bay bathymetry ([#3](https://github.com/cyanheads/elevation-mcp-server/issues/3)).
- **`elevation_get_profile` finer-than-source notice** — keys on the finest source (`resolution_m_range.min_m`) instead of the coarsest, so a mixed-resolution route no longer gets it alongside the coarse-spacing notice ([#6](https://github.com/cyanheads/elevation-mcp-server/issues/6)).
- **Clearance display** — `min_clearance_m`, `min_clearance_ft`, and every terrain point's `clearance_m` show a positive clearance as at least 0.01 m (0.1 ft), so `clear` never displays a 0 clearance; a Fresnel ratio under 0.6 shows as at most 0.59 ([#7](https://github.com/cyanheads/elevation-mcp-server/issues/7)).
